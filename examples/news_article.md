# Mangroves Protected Since 2019, Conservation Group Says

A regional conservation group announced this week that the Green Delta
Reserve's mangrove forest has been under formal protection since 2019, and
that no carbon finance was needed to keep it standing.

The Green Delta Reserve (project GD-2019-004) covers 6,200 hectares about
40 kilometres upriver from the Delta River estuary. It has been a protected
area since 2019, before any carbon project was proposed there.

This article is about the Green Delta Reserve only. It says nothing about
the Delta Mangrove Restoration project (CM-2024-118), which restored
degraded shrimp ponds on a different site in the Delta River estuary.

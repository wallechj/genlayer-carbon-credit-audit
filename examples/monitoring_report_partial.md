# Monitoring Report — Kelp Forest Restoration, Vintage 2024

Auditor: GreenAudit Ltd
Monitoring period: 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024
Methodology: VM0047

## Findings

- Field surveys and sonar mapping confirm 3,100 hectares of restored kelp
  forest in Harbor Bay.
- Measured sequestration for vintage 2024 supports the full 2,000 credits
  claimed: all serials CM-2024-205-0001 to CM-2024-205-2000 are covered by
  monitoring data.
- No monitoring gaps were found. No serials lack monitoring data.

## Additionality

- The restoration would not have happened without carbon finance: the site
  was a barren seabed before the project started in 2023, and no government
  protection order covered this site before the project.
- This finding concerns only the Kelp Forest Restoration project
  (CM-2024-205). Other kelp sites in the region have separate histories and
  are not covered by this report.

## Conclusion

Monitoring supports 2,000 credits for vintage 2024. The auditor makes no
finding about registry accounting: whether the same serials were issued
under another project is a registry matter, not a monitoring matter.

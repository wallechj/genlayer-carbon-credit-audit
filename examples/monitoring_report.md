# Monitoring Report — Delta Mangrove Restoration, Vintage 2024

Auditor: GreenAudit Ltd
Monitoring period: 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024
Methodology: VM0047

## Findings

- Field surveys and satellite imagery confirm 12,400 hectares of restored
  mangrove in the Delta River estuary.
- Measured sequestration for vintage 2024 supports the full 10,000 credits
  claimed: all serials CM-2024-118-0001 to CM-2024-118-10000 are covered by
  monitoring data.
- No monitoring gaps were found. No serials lack monitoring data.

## Additionality

- The restoration would not have happened without carbon finance: the site
  was degraded shrimp ponds before the project started in 2023, and no
  government protection order covered this site before the project.
- This finding concerns only the Delta Mangrove Restoration project
  (CM-2024-118). Other mangrove sites in the region, including the Green Delta
  Reserve, have separate histories and are not covered by this report.

## Conclusion

Monitoring supports 10,000 credits for vintage 2024. The auditor makes no
finding about registry accounting: whether the same serials were issued
under another project is a registry matter, not a monitoring matter.
